Policy Effective 28 August 2026

Privacy, without a data detour.

The CLI

Data Engine Switchboard runs on your computer. It reads only the configuration, transformation modules, and fixture paths you provide. It does not upload fixture contents, reports, code, filenames, or usage data. The CLI has no analytics or telemetry.

The website

The documentation site has no advertising, behavioral analytics, third-party fonts, or tracking scripts. Its service worker may cache public site files on your device so documentation remains available offline.

License data

If you purchase or restore the optional Field Kit, the site stores your license token and a time-stamped validity result in your browser’s local storage. The token is sent only to api.sociobot.in for verification, at most once per day after the first check. Remove the site’s local storage in browser settings to erase it from that device.

Purchases

Sociobot/Dodo is the merchant of record and handles checkout, receipts, payment data, and refunds under its own policies. This site never receives or stores card details.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the repository issue tracker linked from the product page. Do not include datasets, license tokens, or other secrets in an issue.
